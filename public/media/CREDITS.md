## The licence terms that bear on this site

The Pexels licence forbids four things. Three are irrelevant here (selling
unaltered copies as prints, redistributing on another stock site, using an
image as a trademark). The fourth is not:

> Identifiable people may not appear in a bad light or in a way that is
> offensive, and imagery must not imply endorsement of a product by the people
> or brands shown in it.

Several of these photographs show identifiable people. The site therefore
describes them generically in alt text — "an engineer in a safety helmet", not
a named individual — and no caption presents a stock photograph as a Reispeq
project, a Reispeq employee or a customer site. **Keep that true if the
captions change.**

> Before launch, replace the photographs doing real work — the homepage hero
> and the service tiles — with Reispeq's own site photography. Stock reads as
> stock to the buyers in this market, and it also retires the question above.
> `src/lib/media.ts` is the only place a filename appears, so a swap is one
> line per image.

The screenshots in `public/screens/` are a different matter: those are real
captures of the CertiTrack Plus application and are not stock.
